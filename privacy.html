In short. Runmon reads the runs you record on Strava, with your permission, to feed a virtual pet. It keeps each run's details for seven days, then only the XP it earned. Other players see your pet, never your runs. Nothing is sold, advertised with, or put through any AI. Disconnect and your runs are deleted at once and everything else within four weeks.

Who sees what

  • You see everything about your own pet and your runs from the last seven days.
  • Other players see your pet's name, species, form, level, XP, streak, best streak, badge count and any attack penalty on it. They never see your runs, distances, times, dates or Strava profile.
  • Me, as the admin, can see each pet's name, level, XP and whether it is connected, to keep the game running and to restore a pet that was lost. The admin view shows no runs or distances.
  • Nobody else. Your data is never sold, rented, shared with advertisers or data brokers, or used for analytics, and it is never put into any artificial-intelligence system, including for training.

Why Runmon is allowed to use it

Your consent, which you give by connecting Strava after being told what is collected (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); PDPA consent obligation). You can withdraw it at any time by disconnecting. Keeping the game running and secure once you have joined relies on Runmon's legitimate interest in doing so (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your rights, and how to use them

  • See your data — Settings → Download my data gives you everything your phone holds (the server holds the same save). Email me for anything else.
  • Stop — Settings → Disconnect, or remove Runmon at strava.com/settings/apps. Your runs are deleted at once; the rest within four weeks.
  • Delete — Settings → Delete my pet, or email me to have everything about you erased straight away. I will confirm by email when it is done.
  • Correct, object, or move your data — email me.
  • Complain — to me first, please; and you can always go to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission, or the data-protection authority where you live.

I answer requests within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Strava tokens stay on the server and never reach your phone; your phone holds a signed session instead. The admin view is password-protected and locks out repeated wrong guesses. If there is ever a breach affecting your data, I will tell you and the authorities as the law requires, and Strava within 24 hours.

Children

Runmon is not meant for anyone under 13, and Strava's own age rules apply to connecting.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app will say so. If Runmon ever wants to collect something new from Strava, it will ask you first.
