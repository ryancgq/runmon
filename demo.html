Runmon · sandbox build

Every form, one tap away

The real game earns its five forms over about 1,500 km. This build hands them to you straight away, so artwork and pacing can be judged in a minute.

Open the sandbox
  • Tap the DEMO badge, then any of the five forms to jump there
  • “Skip a day” walks the pet down through all six moods
  • Runs on its own save slot — your real pet is never touched
  • Everything else behaves exactly like the real game

For real GPS you still need this page over HTTPS on a phone.
On a desktop, switch on Simulated GPS in Settings.
