Deleted pets

Pets players deleted themselves, kept with the Strava account they belonged to for four weeks and then erased for good — Strava's rules give 30 days. Restoring one puts it back on that account the next time they open the app; they need a pet of their own first (any egg) for it to replace.

Automatic backups

One every night, kept for 14 days: each pet's name, species, level and XP — no runs and no distances. A player who is erased is taken out of every one of them. To put a player back, open them and pick a day under Backup.

Strava webhook

Strava tells Runmon when a player removes it on Strava's own site, or deletes or hides a run, so both are cleared within Strava's 48 hours. Press Subscribe once; it stays on.
