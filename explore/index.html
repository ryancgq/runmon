Runmon Explore

Take your pet off the pet screen and out into the world. Fight the wild pets you meet, and take on Sir Uwaaarghhhh on his hill.

Level
  • Move with your left thumb, anywhere on the left half of the screen.
  • Skills are the fight: your pet's own moves at its level, live, each on its cooldown. Damage and effects are the game's battle numbers; one battle turn is 1.4 seconds.
  • 👊 Attack is mostly a dash: its swipe barely scratches. Nothing he swings can land on you mid-dash, and it cuts a skill short once the skill has landed.
  • Together: everyone with this link open is in the same world. Take him on at the same time and his health is shared.
  • Open PvP: out in the world, other players' pets can fight yours, and yours theirs. While anyone is fighting him, nobody can touch them.
  • Sit by the campfire to heal. Berries heal too.

Nothing here reads or writes your real pet.
